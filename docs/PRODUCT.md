# UNTOLD — Product specification

**Tagline:** Every story has a place.

## What it is
UNTOLD is a privacy-conscious digital space for expression, human connection, reflection and voluntary social discovery. It is not a dating app, therapy service, gossip forum or conventional engagement-driven social network.

## Product pillars
- **Expression:** write without pressure.
- **Connection:** encounter experiences without needing a public identity.
- **Reflection:** notice your own thoughts with consent.
- **Discovery:** explore perspectives through voluntary participation.

## The nine spaces
| Space | Purpose | Standalone status |
|---|---|---|
| THE VOID | Private journal; explicit optional publishing | Independent |
| THE UNSENT | Private letters that need not be sent | Independent |
| SOMEONE, SOMEWHERE | Moderated anonymous story feed | Independent, needs moderation |
| THE OTHER SIDE | Conversations with listen / understand / advice modes | Independent, needs moderation |
| THE TEA ROOM | Harmless funny confessions | Independent, strict anti-harassment rules |
| THE MIRROR | Opt-in reflection on one's own writing | Depends on explicit data opt-in |
| THE SILENT MATCH | Topic-based discovery with mutual consent | Independent, needs consent and safety controls |
| THE HUMAN EXPERIMENT | Voluntary polls and disclosed social experiments | Independent, requires transparent methodology |
| THE TIME CAPSULE | Thoughts delivered back on a chosen date | Independent, needs reliable scheduler |

## First release scope
- Product shell, navigation and responsive dashboard.
- Account and secure session foundation.
- THE VOID and THE UNSENT with server-side ownership checks.
- SOMEONE, SOMEWHERE with moderation queue.
- Reporting, blocking, deletion and moderator audit trail.

## Core user journey
Landing → explore or create account → dashboard → choose any space → understand visibility → write or participate → save/share with explicit consent → manage, edit or delete.

## Product-wide rules
1. Private content is private by default.
2. Private writing and public posts are separate data and permission paths.
3. Anonymous to other members is not equivalent to technically untraceable.
4. No sale of private writing; no ad targeting from private writing.
5. No silent AI analysis or model training on personal writing.
6. No diagnosis, clinical claims or promise of therapy.
7. Safety, reporting, blocking, moderation, appeals and deletion are launch requirements.
8. Experiments are opt-in and disclose procedures, simulated stimuli and limitations.
